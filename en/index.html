Ideas from everywhere.

KOLA is the kola nut, shared in West Africa to welcome guests and open a conversation. KOLA Idées publishes books in the same spirit: ideas that travel from one culture to another, from one language to another, to help us grow and move past our limits.

KOLA also means "to grow" in Lingala and Tshiluba (DR Congo).
