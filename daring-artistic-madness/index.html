About the book

Daring Artistic Madness — Book One: The Manifesto is a call to examine the invisible rules that limit your imagination. It doesn’t promise a magic transformation. It offers a different way of looking at the hesitations, the blocks, and the need for validation that keep an idea from ever taking shape.

Daring Artistic Madness is Book One of a planned trilogy on creative freedom. This English edition currently covers Book One; the rest of the trilogy is still part of the ongoing editorial project.
